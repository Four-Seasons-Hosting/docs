---
title: "Fix \"Authentication Servers Are Down\" in Minecraft"
description: "What to do when Minecraft says the authentication servers are down for maintenance."
---

`Failed to login: Authentication servers are down. Please try again later` comes from Mojang's login service, not from your server. The client could not verify your account, so no server will let you in until it can.

## Fix It

1. Check whether Mojang has an outage. If many players report the same error, wait for it to be resolved.
2. Restart Minecraft and the launcher, and sign out and back in to your Microsoft account.
3. Restart your router so you get a fresh connection.
4. If you use a VPN, turn it off. If you do not, try one: some networks block the login service.
5. If none of this helps and there is no outage, contact [Minecraft Support](https://help.minecraft.net).

## Server Owners

Leave `online-mode=true` in `server.properties`. Turning it off lets anyone join under any username, including yours, and does not fix the login problem for players.
