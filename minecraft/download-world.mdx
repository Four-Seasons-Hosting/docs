---
title: "How To Download A Minecraft Server's World"
---

1. Log in to the game panel at `gamepanel.fourseasonshosting.com` and open your server.
2. Open the **Files** page.
3. Find your world folder its name matches the `level-name` value in `server.properties` (usually `world`). Right-click the folder and select **Archive**.
4. An archive of the world folder will appear in the file list. Right-click it and select **Download**.

Once you have the archive, extract it and place the world folder into your singleplayer saves directory. If you have trouble opening the archive, use a tool such as **7-Zip** or **WinRAR**.

For large worlds, downloading over **SFTP** is more reliable than the panel download, your SFTP credentials are under the server's **Settings** page.
