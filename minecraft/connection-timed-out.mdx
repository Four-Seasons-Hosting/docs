---
title: "Fix \"Connection Timed Out: getsockopt\" in Minecraft"
description: "Why Minecraft says Connection timed out: getsockopt when joining a server, and how to fix it."
---

`Connection timed out: getsockopt` means your Minecraft client sent a connection request and never got an answer. The server may be offline, the address may be wrong, or something between you and the server is dropping the traffic.

## Check the Server First

1. Log in to the game panel at `gamepanel.fourseasonshosting.com` and open your server.
2. On the **Console** page, confirm the server is running and the console shows `Done` after startup.
3. Copy the address shown on the panel, including the port after the colon, and paste it into Minecraft exactly as shown.

If the server is stopped or crashing on startup, fix that first. The console output above the crash explains why.

## Check Your Own Connection

If the server is running and other players can join, the problem is on your side.

- **Restricted network:** School, work and public Wi-Fi often block game traffic. Try a mobile hotspot or another network.
- **Firewall:** On Windows, open **Windows Security** > **Firewall & network protection** > **Allow an app through firewall** and make sure `javaw.exe` is allowed on both **Private** and **Public** networks.
- **VPN:** A VPN can bypass a restrictive network, or be the cause of the timeout. Try connecting with it on and off.
- **Router:** Restart your router if every server times out.

## Still Timing Out

If other players can join and you have tried another network, open a support ticket with your server address and a screenshot of the error. In rare cases a route to one node is affected, and we can move your server to a different node, which changes its address.
