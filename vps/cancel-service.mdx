---
title: "How to Cancel A Service"
---

1. Login in to your client account on the [Client Portal](https://fourseasonshosting.com/client/clientarea.php)
2. Click on "Services" \> "My Services" at top bar on Navigation Bar
3. You'll see the list of services, click on Service that you want to cancel.

![b64u-MDE5ZTFhMGQtYmRhOC03YzQ5LWFhM2UtNzY1ZmI3Mzg5MGYw.png](/images/166c68ea-3fd5-4095-8821-880dbaebba00.png)

This is what it should look like when in the right area!

4. Click on Request Cancellation and follow the prompts on the screen

<Warning>
  **Immediate** means it will Terminated at the **next** 8pm EST
</Warning>

<Danger>
  When a service is canceled ALL SERVER DATA WILL BE DELETED AND **_UNRECOVERABLE_**
</Danger>
