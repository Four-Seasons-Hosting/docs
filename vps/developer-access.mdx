---
title: "Developers and your VPS"
---

<Warning>
  📌 We **RECOMMENDED** that you never allow access to your VPS Administrator account unless you 100% trust the admins or developers
</Warning>

For allowing access for developers or admins into your VPS would be best by adding them as users on the VPS

On your VPS, please go to Control Panel \> User Accounts \> User Accounts \> Manage Another Accounts \> Add A User Account
